PAUL ATREIDES / A READING JOURNAL

From heir to legend. From legend to warning.

Six long-form reading essays follow Paul through Frank Herbert’s Dune, Dune Messiah and Children of Dune. Each essay offers at least 15 minutes of reading and three visual aids. Plot context is sourced; the sections labelled “Reading the theme” are our interpretation. This is book commentary, separate from Project Dune’s game mechanics.

Spoiler notice: the collection covers Paul’s entire arc in the first three novels. Each article identifies its book scope before you reveal the text. Read in chronicle order, or select the book you have finished.
