The current in-game exchange

Player-posted listings are spice harvesting contracts: 15% of earnings go to the worker, 85% to the sponsor. They are distinct from item sell orders. The normal spice sale is a standing sale to the economy at 15 Solaris per unit by default, with the Imperial bonus and Trust levy applied in game.

The website is a read-only market board. Complete trades or accept contracts inside Minecraft. It does not spend Solaris or hold items.
