01 / ARRAKDUIN FIELD NOTES

Nothing here
comes easily.

Water is precious. Spice is power. Every expedition begins with a reclaimer suit and ends with a story.

01

Walk a living desert

Dunes, oasis trees, cave rockhavens and darkstone formations up to three plateaus high. Find shelter before the Arrakduin sun finds you.

02

Harvest the wealth of worlds

Use the tagged Spice Harvest Shovel to collect spice. Sell for Dunemarks, fill KOOPKRING quotas, or work a player-sponsored spice contract.

03

Prepare for Dune Maw

A desert-skinned Sniffer carries the sandworm’s form, with native zombie AI. Survive the encounter; its death leaves a rich spice field.
