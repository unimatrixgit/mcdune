ILLUSTRATED FACTION & PLANET ATLAS
Kaladam

The hawk above the water

House Aerdrecht

Kaladam’s seas and green landscapes give the Aerdrecht card its water-world identity: a contrast to the dry struggle for spice on Arrakduin.

Planet profile
Jungle & water terrain
Environment
Water level 72 · trees enabled
In the plugin
Movement refreshes Speed I; a matching full reclaimer suit adds four hearts.

Giedermark

Iron, shadow and power

House Harkveen

Dark stone, red heraldry and industrial imagery evoke the Harkveen presence. In dunerealms.com, their harsh homeworld complements a faction built around combat.

Planet profile
Swamp · blackstone · deepslate
Environment
Water level 63 · swamp pools
In the plugin
Player-caused combat damage +15%; a matching full suit refreshes Strength II.

Kaiterhof

The weight of the Concord

House Korenholt

Imperial gold and the lion motif frame Korenholt’s seat on Kaiterhof. The artwork imagines courtly grandeur; the released world is a procedural plains profile.

Planet profile
Plains · grass · dirt
Environment
Flower placement enabled · no generated trees
In the plugin
Protection III kit armor; entity-caused incoming combat damage reduced by 15%.

Arrakduin

Water is life. The maker watches.

Vrijduin

Rockhavens, reclaimer suits and the great sandworms shape the desert identity. Spice draws travelers into the dunes, while water and shelter make survival possible.

Planet profile
Desert · spice fields · rock plateaus
Environment
Heat exposure · finite water · sandworm encounters
In the plugin
Two spice units per harvested deposit; brief Resistance I while moving on Arrakduin.

Sigmadraken IV

Secrets beneath the snow

House Oordwijk

Oordwijk adds secrecy and cold-world imagery to dunerealms.com’s setting. Its snowy destination and concealment equipment form this plugin’s interpretation of the House.

Planet profile
Snowy plains · snow blocks · deepslate
Environment
Base height 72 · no custom freezing meter
In the plugin
Matching full suit enables five-second Shadow Step; kit includes an explosive charge.

The planetary network

Routes among the stars

Vaerkring

The Compact motif connects the worlds through celestial charts and Routekeeper imagery. In the plugin, Compact NPCs connect players to travel; the Compact is not a selectable player faction.

Planet profile
Arrakduin · Kaladam · Giedermark
Environment
Kaiterhof · Sigmadraken IV
In the plugin
DuneEss provides five-world travel. Compact dispatches share recorded Arrakduin sightings after the 15-minute warning.

Travel service · no separate Compact homeworld configured
