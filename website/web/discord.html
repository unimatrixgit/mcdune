A SHARED CODE FOR THE CARAVAN

Before you join the conversation

  • Be respectful: no harassment, hate speech or threats.
  • Protect privacy: never post passwords, account tokens or personal information.
  • Keep conversations in the appropriate channel; avoid spam and unsolicited advertising.
  • Report problems privately through Support. Do not publish private ticket details.
  • Follow the server rules and staff guidance. Disagreements can be raised calmly through Support.
