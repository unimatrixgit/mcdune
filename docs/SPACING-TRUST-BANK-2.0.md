# Spacing Trust Bank 2.0 — complete fictional banking scope

Status: design and engineering acceptance criteria, NOT a live service. All currency, credit, rates, and contracts are fictional in-game Dunemarks (DM); no real-money deposits, withdrawals, cash-out, real-world credit reporting or debt enforcement.

## Accounts and customer services
Personal current and savings, joint, company, clan, five House, Landsraad, Imperial and Spacing Guild accounts. Unique fictional IBAN-style numbers with MOD-97 and reserved 979 official prefix. Account lifecycle: open, approve, activate, freeze, unfreeze, close and archive. Co-owner invitations and scoped revocable mandates (view, deposit, pay, manage, approve) with limits, expiry, maker-checker for sensitive transactions and role transitions on elections. Beneficiaries, payment requests, standing orders, recurring payments, direct-debit mandates with revocation, escrow, scheduled transfers, batch payroll, searchable statements, OGM and free references, receipts, exports and dispute handling.

## Core banking ledger
Use one authoritative transactional double-entry general ledger. Every posting balances to zero, is immutable, has idempotency key and source event, account epoch, currency, timestamps and authorized actor. Never silently mint DM through a secondary plugin or website. Separate available and reserved funds; prevent double spending, enforce nonnegative ordinary accounts except authorized loan-repayment overdrafts, explicit overdraft limits, transaction locks and concurrent-request fencing. Track all income and expenses, including fictional 21% Imperial VAT on eligible commerce, without taxing own-account transfers. Preserve historical ledgers through prelaunch reset; archive old epoch and reconcile opening balances.

## Loans and credit products
- Personal installment loans for approved gameplay purposes.
- Corporate term loans and revolving credit facilities.
- Application -> eligibility and affordability -> offer -> explicit acceptance -> approval -> disbursement -> repayment schedule -> closure.
- Store principal, annual rate, term, fixed/variable rule, interest accrual basis, installment schedule, grace policy, fees, status, contract version and signatures. Money calculations use integer cents/decimal, with final-installment rounding correction.
- Automated scheduled repayment with idempotent run key; early repayment, partial payments, rescheduling, missed payments, default and voluntary settlement. Never repeatedly charge on retry.
- If the scheduled installment exceeds available funds, permit the borrower's designated loan-linked current account to become negative by the unpaid difference, only up to a documented loan overdraft cap. Example: 500 DM available, 1,000 DM installment => -500 DM. This is an authorized loan repayment posting, not permission for ordinary transfers, purchases or Exchange trades to overspend.
- Incoming funds first reduce the negative balance. Track the overdraft separately from remaining loan principal to prevent counting the same debt twice. Reject or flag any installment beyond the authorized negative limit, with a clear overdue status; never silently mint funds. Block additional borrowing while materially overdue.
- **Maximum five active loans per borrower account:** At most 5 simultaneously open loans per personal or corporate borrowing account. Count pending-disbursement, active, overdue and restructured loans against the cap; closed, fully settled or cancelled applications do not count. Enforce the limit atomically under account-level locking to prevent simultaneous applications bypassing it. Additional borrowing to cover an overdue installment or late fee is permitted only if the borrower remains under the five-loan cap, the new loan passes a fresh affordability/credit check and disbursement is applied directly to the overdue debt. Such refinancing must not silently waive an already assessed fee, erase an existing overdue record, or reset the grace period. A refinanced borrower may be removed from the credit blacklist only once all arrears are settled. Never allow a borrower to bypass the cap by using multiple linked personal accounts; also enforce a borrower-wide cap by verified Minecraft UUID.\n- **40% late fee:** After the published grace period expires, charge a one-time fictional late fee equal to 40% of the **unpaid overdue installment** (not the original total loan, remaining principal, or entire account balance). Example: 1,000 DM installment unpaid -> 400 DM fee, total arrears 1,400 DM. Each installment may incur this fee at most once, enforced by a unique loan-installment fee key and an idempotent ledger entry. No daily, repeated or compounding late fees; do not charge again on retries. Disclose the fee before accepting the loan and in reminder notices, and allow correction of erroneous charges. Late fees remain in-game only and must not create real-world obligations. For younger players, require a conservative maximum total fee cap and a clear grace period before enabling this rule.\n- **Automatic debt recovery from incoming funds:** When a blacklisted or overdue borrower's designated current account receives Dunemarks, the bank automatically applies available incoming funds toward overdue loan installments and any loan-linked negative balance, up to the outstanding debt. Recovery is an atomic, idempotent ledger posting in the same authoritative transaction as the incoming credit, or a safely serialized follow-up if the payment rail cannot support it. Never collect more than the outstanding amount; do not seize funds from joint, House, clan or company accounts merely because the borrower has access. No deductions from unrelated accounts or real-world payments. Provide receipts, audit entries, visible remaining debt, dispute handling and automatic credit-blacklist removal once arrears are fully cleared. Prevent repeated deductions on retry, race conditions and double-counting of overdraft versus loan principal. Define repayment allocation order (overdraft, overdue interest, overdue principal) explicitly before launch.\n- **Fictional bank credit blacklist:** after a missed due date and a clearly disclosed grace period, an unpaid loan automatically marks the borrower as `credit_blacklisted` in Spacing Trust. Blacklisted players cannot take new loans, credit lines or act as guarantors; their normal gameplay, earned funds, existing accounts and ability to receive payments remain available. Repaying all overdue installments clears the blacklist automatically (unless a separate documented fraud restriction applies). Notify the borrower of the due date, grace period, blacklist reason and reinstatement conditions; allow staff review of incorrect entries. This is an in-game bank-only flag, not a Minecraft ban or real-world credit record.
- Separate principal, interest, accrued interest, write-offs and recoveries in the general ledger. Display effective total fictional cost before acceptance.
- Lending policy: bank liquidity/reserve rules and an explicit owner-controlled money creation policy. Default to **fully funded lending**: debit bank lending reserve and credit borrower; repayments restore reserve, interest transfers to bank earnings. No free balance creation. Credit facilities reserve committed funds.
- No loan purchase with real money, no debt tied to real-world identities, no punitive debt traps, no negative real-world consequences. For young players: conservative limits, cooling-off, clear disclosures, no collection harassment, no real-money collateral.

## Borrowing limits by institution
- **Personal borrowers:** additional borrowing and refinancing are permitted subject to affordability and funded lending reserves. Refinancing can settle overdue installments and the one-time 40% late fee, then remove the in-game credit blacklist only once arrears have actually been paid. A new loan must never silently erase an outstanding debt or fee.
- **Companies:** maximum **two active loans** per company, counted across corporate term loans and refinancing facilities. A refinancing replacement must atomically settle or close the replaced loan before the slot is released. Company directors cannot evade the cap using another authorized signer.
- **Government:** **unlimited concurrent loan count** for the Imperial Treasury, Landsraad, the five official House treasuries and the Spacing Guild's official institutional treasury. This is not unlimited money: each loan requires documented authority, actual available bank funding, repayment terms, audited ledger entries and public monthly reporting. A newly elected officeholder inherits the institution's obligations and cannot move them into a personal account.
- Company-owned Spacing Guild commercial subsidiaries remain subject to the **company** cap unless they are explicitly the official institutional treasury. Clan treasuries are not government accounts.
- All credit restrictions, delinquency and automatic recovery rules remain applicable unless a separately approved, audited government borrowing policy explicitly provides otherwise.

## Risk, treasury and administration
Bank capital/reserves, liquidity coverage, daily reconciliation, exposure by player/company, concentration limits, anti-wash trading, suspicious transfers, loan-loss provisioning, debt aging, write-offs and authorized audit. Support queues, disputed transactions, charge reversals (new compensating postings, never editing old entries), account freezes, dual approval for large adjustments, staff segregation of duties and immutable security logs.

## Investments and integration
DuneHub and in-game interfaces share bank service. Exchange, Kit Exchange, Spacing Guild shares, DRX20, company registry, contracts, imperial taxation, House treasuries and monthly public statements use the same authoritative ledger. Company dividends and share purchases are debits from real funded accounts. Company loans cannot directly buy issuer's own shares or create wash trades. All settlement references carry OGM plus separate unique transaction ID.

## Explicit non-goals
No mortgages, property-backed lending, property foreclosure, real licensed banking, Belgian IBAN claims, real-world credit, real-money interest, financial investment promises, or unrestricted creation of Dunemarks.
